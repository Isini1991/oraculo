Oráculo is an independent label in Spain, co-founded by Nico Cabañas. A home for darkwave enthusiasts and record collectors — where electronic sound becomes something you can hold.

Plastical jewels. No ordinary objects.From Spain. Beyond borders.
03 — In focus / Nico Cabañas

A NEW NAME.
THE SAME
RESTLESSNESS.

From Synths Versus Me and Almax und Forte to Estado de Bienestar. Oráculo and Ombra Festival co-founder Nico Cabañas opens another chapter in his musical life.

His debut, La Escorpiona y Guateque Punk, brings EBM, industrial dub, darkwave, breakbeat and jazz into an uncompromising, analog-driven world.
